The Script of Your Life — Authorized Final Draft ✦ NEW45,000 gp
Wondrous book · Legendary · Requires attunement
A velvet-bound screenplay bearing your name beneath a worryingly long list of executive producers. Most pages are blank. One scene is not: a moment of triumph that has not happened yet. Consult the margins to discover how to get there. Apparently free will tests extremely well with audiences.
At attunement, the DM writes one specific, achievable future goal for your character into the final scene. You may propose an ambition, but the Script chooses its foretold outcome. The goal must fit the campaign; it cannot guarantee another creature’s feelings or decisions. Only one goal can be active.
Consult up to 3 times per 7 days, no more than once per day. Each consultation requires 10 uninterrupted minutes and answers one question about achieving the foretold goal. The DM provides one truthful, actionable next step: a person to approach, a place to visit, a preparation to make, or an obstacle to address. If no progress is presently possible, it explains the immediate obstacle.
The Script shows a possible route based on current circumstances, not an unavoidable future. It never compels anyone, completes the goal for you, or guarantees safety. If the route changes, later consultations reflect that change.
Once per 30 days, you may spend 1 hour requesting a Rewrite, replacing the active goal with a new DM-approved foretelling. Reattuning, changing owners, or achieving a goal does not reset these limits. After success, further consultations await the next Rewrite.
"You’re the protagonist! Travel, props, supporting cast, and funeral expenses not included."
